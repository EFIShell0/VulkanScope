# VulkanScope 2.0.6 Surface chooser scroll audit

## Scope
- Fix the Surface landing chooser when its destination cards exceed the available viewport height, especially in landscape and navigation-constrained windows.
- Preserve the established overlay, system-navigation and floating-bottom-navigation safe areas.
- Avoid shrinking or hiding Surface destinations to make them fit.

## Requirements
- `SurfaceSectionCards` must remain a single shared chooser for Display & HDR, Surface & color spaces and Presentation.
- The chooser must become vertically scrollable when content exceeds the viewport and remain stationary when all content fits.
- The existing transient status-overlay top inset, Android navigation-bar left/right insets and floating bottom-navigation clearance remain mandatory.
- Scroll-boundary indicators must use the same safe insets and must not render underneath transient overlays or behind the floating navigation bar.
- Desktop pointer-wheel scrolling and focus-group behavior remain available through the shared scroll path.
- No Surface capability semantics, Vulkan query, report/Database schema, native ownership, permission, network endpoint, updater trust rule, scheduler, Android TV key handling or build-chain version may change in this release.

## Implemented design
- `SurfaceSectionCards` now owns a bounded `ScrollState` and places the existing animated destination cards inside a vertically scrollable `Column`.
- The scrollable column keeps the previously established start/top/end/bottom safe-area padding.
- `ExpressiveScrollHints` now mirrors those same safe-area bounds for this chooser.

## Expected evidence
- In landscape on a three-button-navigation handset, the Presentation destination can be scrolled fully into view and activated without being hidden by the bottom navigation bar.
- In larger windows where all three destinations fit, the chooser remains visually unchanged apart from inactive scroll mechanics.
- Transient collection/network/update banners continue to push the chooser downward while visible and release the space when dismissed.
