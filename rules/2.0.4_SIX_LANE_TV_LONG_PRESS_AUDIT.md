# VulkanScope 2.0.4 adaptive six-lane and Android TV long-press audit

## Scope
- Reduce complete collection time further from the validated 2.0.3 one-shot baseline without reintroducing multi-query process reuse or reducing capability/query coverage.
- Add a TV-remote pathway to the existing evidence provenance/actions dialog by holding OK/DPAD_CENTER/Enter on a focused evidence row.

## Performance requirements
- One Vulkan query group per dedicated process lifetime remains mandatory. No process may execute multiple background query groups.
- Automatic background concurrency is resource-adaptive with a hard ceiling of six isolated non-exported private process lanes. Low-RAM/low-memory devices remain capped at two lanes; progressively tighter physical-memory, available-memory, available-ratio and CPU conditions cap capable devices at three, four or five lanes before six is allowed.
- Six-lane execution is allowed only when Android does not classify the device as low-RAM or low-memory, at least eight logical processors are available, physical/available-memory thresholds pass and available-memory ratio remains above the bounded threshold.
- Dynamic work claiming and deterministic original-query-order merge remain unchanged.
- Active Turnip driver paths are resolved once per background collection pass and reused only for that immutable collection snapshot. Driver mutation remains blocked while collection is active.
- Existing one-shot process quiescence, timeout, crash/cancellation recovery, result/timing byte bounds, cache confinement and 60-second background budget remain mandatory.

## Android TV input requirements
- On Android TV, a focused capability/evidence row must open the existing Evidence provenance/actions dialog when the remote OK/DPAD_CENTER/Enter key is held long enough to generate Android long-press/repeat evidence.
- The initial short key-down must not open the dialog. A short OK/Enter press remains available for normal focus/activation behavior.
- After the long-press gesture is recognized, repeated activation events and the corresponding release are consumed so the same hold does not trigger duplicate actions.
- The TV behavior is additive to touch long-press and accessibility custom actions. Desktop/Googlebook/ARC application-owned secondary-click quick-menu suppression from 2.0.2 remains mandatory.

## Correctness and release boundary
- Vulkan native collector and registry/generated query coverage remain unchanged from 2.0.3. No capability is removed, inferred or downgraded for performance.
- Report/Database schema, Unknown/Unavailable/Unsupported semantics, updater trust, permissions, network policy and ABI/build-chain versions remain unchanged.
- Production changes are limited to MainActivity scheduler/input handling, two additional private background service endpoints/classes, manifest declarations and release identity.
