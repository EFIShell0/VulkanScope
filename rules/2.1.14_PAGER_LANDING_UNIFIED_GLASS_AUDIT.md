## Failing-before-fix evidence
- The supplied 8.646-second 2.1.13 runtime video shows the page control remaining as a page-level overlay even after the ordinary list position has returned, producing a visually detached landing instead of rejoining the list item.
- 2.1.13 retained the pager overlay at zero handoff progress. The list therefore never became the visual owner of the normal-flow pager.
- The lazy item owned its measured pager-height state. Lazy disposal could recreate that state from the 72 dp estimate instead of the last measured value.
- The app chrome blur radius was 24 dp while the pager join blur radius was 26 dp, creating a visibly different backdrop at the header/join boundary.
- The pager join glass height was progress-interpolated independently of the moving pager bottom, so the frosted extension could look cut away from the pager during handoff.

## Repair
- Normal flow renders `CollectionPager` inside the lazy item. A same-height spacer replaces it only while the page-level overlay exclusively owns the join/pinned state.
- Overlay ownership begins only inside the bounded header-distance join region. At zero progress the overlay is removed and the in-flow pager owns the exact lazy position.
- The owning `VulkanLazyPage` retains measured pager heights in a keyed state map so lazy-item disposal does not reset landing geometry.
- Direct pager-item offset remains authoritative when available. Immediate previous/next visible items reconstruct the natural pager offset across the narrow composition gap before direct pager visibility returns.
- Pager overlay translation remains scroll-derived with no whole-pager tween. The header boundary is the only clamp.
- Pager join blur radius is 24 dp, matching the app header/compact navigation blur. The same uniform `VulkanGlassTint` is used and gradient tinting remains absent.
- The bounded join glass reaches the moving pager bottom plus 12 dp while ownership is active. The ordinary page still replays the crisp retained layer and is never globally blurred.
- The upper scroll indicator keeps the coordinated header → pager → transient status → indicator stack and its existing bounded 220 ms position animation.

## Performance and safety boundary
- No PixelCopy, framebuffer readback, bitmap capture, per-frame bitmap allocation or unbounded blur path is introduced.
- No manifest, permission, endpoint, dependency, native/JNI, Vulkan registry/generated data, report/Database/export semantic, Turnip handling, ABI or packaged artwork change is introduced.
