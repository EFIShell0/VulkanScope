# VulkanScope 0.32.9 Turnip SAF and HDR asset audit

- The Turnip document-launch path is restored from the known-working 0.32.4 implementation without reinterpretation: AndroidX OpenDocument and MIME candidates application/zip, application/octet-stream, */*.
- The 0.32.7/0.32.8 picker-in-flight and narrowed-MIME experiments are not used by the launcher in this release.
- Post-selection package trust remains the current hardened path: bounded extraction, zip-slip/canonical containment, required meta.json/schemaVersion, exact declared libraryName, private storage and non-empty .so validation.
- Driver-import commit still refuses to proceed while Vulkan collection is active; Settings keeps Turnip actions disabled during collection.
- Dolby Vision is stored as an actual white RGBA wordmark for Compose rendering.
- HDR10+ stores a white wordmark while preserving the colored 10+ artwork. No runtime tint or black-wordmark asset is used for those two cards.
- Vulkan 1.4.360 query/export/Database schema-v3 behavior is unchanged from 0.32.8.
