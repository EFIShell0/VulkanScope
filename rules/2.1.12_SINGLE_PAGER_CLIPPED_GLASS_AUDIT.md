## Failing-before-fix evidence
- The supplied 8.04-second runtime video and screenshot show the capability cards blurred outside the intended top glass region.
- The same evidence shows two pager cards during the handoff because 2.1.11 rendered a normal-flow pager and a second overlay pager and crossfaded between them.
- The 2.1.11 lazy-item `DisposableEffect` removed the pager registration when the anchor composition was disposed, so the overlay lifetime was still coupled to lazy-item retention.
- The visible one-pixel glass/header edge produced a separate joining line instead of a continuous surface.

## Repair
- The top glass container clips its retained blurred-layer draw to its own bounds before tint composition, so the ordinary capability page remains crisp.
- The lazy collection-pager item is now a measured-height placeholder only. Exactly one `CollectionPager` is drawn by `VulkanLazyPage`; while the anchor is visible it follows the anchor's live offset and when the anchor has scrolled past it clamps at the live header boundary.
- Pager registration is owned by `VulkanLazyPage` rather than the lazy item's disposal lifetime. A no-pager cleanup item removes stale registration when pagination is no longer required, and layout-item-count matching remains a generation guard.
- Pager measurement feeds the placeholder height so the list does not collapse or jump when font/layout metrics change.
- The glass and header bottom hairlines were removed. The glass height still grows only by the bounded pager lane amount.
- The upper scroll indicator consumes one animated combined top-stack inset; transient status remains positioned from the animated pager lane.

## Performance and safety boundary
- No PixelCopy, screenshot bitmap, `toImageBitmap`, repeated bitmap allocation or new network/storage capability is introduced.
- The retained source/blur GraphicsLayer path and one API-31 blur RenderEffect are preserved, with the blur output now clipped to the glass bounds.
- Native/JNI Vulkan collection, registry data, report/Database schema, Turnip handling, manifest, dependencies and ABI set remain predecessor-equivalent.
