## Demonstrated failure
The supplied Windows `:app:assembleRelease` run completes native builds for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64 and then fails at `:app:compileReleaseKotlin`. The Kotlin compiler reports seven unsupported escape diagnostics across four regular-expression literals: three positions in `AdvancedAnalysis.kt:273`, one at `AdvancedAnalysis.kt:312`, one at `AdvancedAnalysis.kt:321`, and two at `MainActivity.kt:5328`.

A standalone Kotlin compiler probe over the same four predecessor literals reproduces illegal-escape failures for `\s`, `\.`, and `\d`.
