## Demonstrated UI regression
The supplied real-device screenshots show three concrete usability regressions in 0.80.14: the permanent right-side indicator lane narrows page content, the Overview Explore strip visibly clips later controls at phone width, and the flat detail-dialog evidence list loses the grouped visual hierarchy the user preferred. These are presentation/usability defects; no Vulkan evidence defect is inferred from the screenshots.

## Repair
0.80.15 overlays scroll hints on content instead of reserving width and increases arrow icon geometry to 24dp. Up is placed at the viewport top-center and Down at the viewport bottom-center. At the absolute top only the lower Down hint is eligible, in the middle the upper Up and lower Down hints are eligible, at the absolute bottom only the upper Up hint is eligible, and non-scrollable content has no indicator. Hints remain visible while scrolling, remain briefly visible after movement, then fade out; renewed gesture, fling or programmatic scrolling fades them back in. No sliding or position animation is introduced.

The Extension/Format detail Dialog retains a custom Material 3 surface and sticky Close action but restores compact grouped tonal evidence cards with a subtle outline. Narrow, enlarged-text and long-content cases stack label above value; sufficiently wide short rows may remain two-column. Dialog margins, width and body height are Configuration-bounded for portrait/landscape phone and tablet layouts.

Overview Explore and Quick access are width-aware wrapping grids so phone layouts do not rely on a clipped horizontal destination strip or a fixed four-column grid.

## Evidence boundaries
Official Android Compose documentation was rechecked before editing. `LazyListState.isScrollInProgress` reports gesture, fling and programmatic scrolling, and `AnimatedVisibility` with fade transitions is the platform visibility-animation mechanism used for the transient indicators. No new dependency or runtime network path is introduced.

The targeted source verifier fails on immutable 0.80.14 and passes on 0.80.15. The behavioral state model covers top/middle/bottom/non-scrollable direction and viewport placement, initial hint, scrolling visibility, idle disappearance, resumed-scrolling reappearance, responsive card stacking and adaptive Overview grid columns. Negative mutations cover both functional and placement regressions plus an unrelated-documentation false-positive control.

Android compilation and real-device reproduction of the new layout remain separate evidence classes and are NOT EXECUTED by the packaging environment unless separately recorded.
